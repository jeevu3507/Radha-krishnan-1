Why this platform

Today, every TN award scheme is re-implemented from scratch on TNEMIS — taking 6–8 person-months per award with high regression risk. UAMP turns the form, the workflow, the evaluation rubric, and the committee structure into configurable metadata, so a new award scheme can be launched by an admin in days, not months.

≤ 10 daysto launch a new award (from 6–8 months)

Platform principles

Schema-as-data

Forms live in PostgreSQL JSONB, not in code. Versioned and immutable — publishing v4 never invalidates in-flight v3 submissions.

Workflow-as-JSON

Approval graphs, SLAs, committees, and reducers are all declarative. Built in a visual workflow builder, validated, sandbox-previewed, and published.

Bilingual by construction

Every label, hint, error, and option carries both English and Tamil. Noto Sans Tamil baked into the design system. WCAG 2.1 AA accessible.

Tamper-evident audit

Append-only audit ledger with Merkle-chained row hashes. Every state change is signed, IP-stamped, and legally defensible.
